Questions, answered.

Anything else, call the shop. A person picks up.

How long does an order take?

Every piece is decorated after you order it. Most are ready within ten business days, and never more than two weeks. You get an email when it is done.

Can I pick up instead of paying for shipping?

Yes. Choose in-store pickup at checkout and collect it at Bullshirt on Route 1 during business hours. Pickup is free.

Can I add a name or number?

On hoodies, crewnecks, and tees, yes. Open the item and use the designer to set the back text before adding it to the cart.

Does the school get anything from each sale?

A share of every order goes back to York High School athletics through the store's commission. The percentage is set with the school.

What if the size is wrong?

Decorated items are made to order, so check the size chart on each product before buying. If something arrives damaged, the shop makes it right.
