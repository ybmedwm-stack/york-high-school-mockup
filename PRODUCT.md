All facts below are inferred from the brief and public sources unless marked confirmed. Dave directed the session to make the decisions; no interview was run. [inferred] marks each inference.

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, deployed to GitHub Pages under ybmedwm-stack. Chosen because it is the Studio stack recorded in Dave's global workflow and because the artifact is a design mockup whose CSS and sections will later be ported into DecoNetwork's theming surfaces (Customize Template HTML, Customize Template CSS, Pages editor Html widgets).

## Users

[inferred] Parents, students, alumni, and staff of York High School (York, Maine) buying spirit wear: hoodies, tees, caps, quarter-zips. They arrive from a school newsletter, the athletics site, or a social post, usually on a phone, and want to pick an item, choose size and color, and check out in one sitting.

## Product Purpose

A branded storefront for York High School spirit wear, fulfilled by Bullshirt Custom Apparel (York, ME) through its DecoNetwork fulfillment center. Success is a visitor adding an item to the DecoNetwork cart within seconds of landing, and the school's booster or athletics contact seeing a store that feels like York's, not a templated print-shop page.

## Positioning

[inferred] Every item is printed by hand in York by the shop that has outfitted the town's teams since 2002, with no order windows and free in-store pickup. A national print-on-demand store cannot truthfully claim local production or walk-in pickup.

## Operating Context

- Commerce, product designer, cart, and checkout live on DecoNetwork at a store URL of the form `<slug>.bullshirt.com`. This mockup's product cards deep-link to placeholder product URLs; real DecoNetwork product IDs replace them at port time.
- The mockup is the design artifact for the premium storefront playbook in the Bullshirt project folder. It is never the deployed store.
- Products are loaded in DecoNetwork from supplier catalogs (SanMar, S&S Activewear, Gildan). Decoration is screen print or embroidery.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static page. No server, no accounts, no real cart. Every "add to cart" style action is a link out to DecoNetwork.
- Product imagery is authored placeholder illustration until real DecoNetwork mockup renders exist. Labeled as such.
- Prices shown are placeholder figures for layout, not quotes. Undecided: real product list, prices, commission, store type (continuous vs. open/close window).
- Undecided: whether this store sits beside the existing `yhswrestling.bullshirt.com` affiliate store or replaces it.

## Brand Commitments

- School: York High School, York, Maine. Mascot: Wildcats. Colors: Columbia blue and white (Wikipedia, MaxPreps). No official logo file on hand; the mockup uses an authored wordmark, to be replaced by the school's mark.
- Binding visual reference from the brief: pull design elements from https://voltsconsulting.com/ (near-black ground, white type, one electric accent, pill controls, ticker banner, collage hero, tagged work grid, count-up stats, FAQ accordion).
- Fulfillment credit: Bullshirt Custom Apparel, 1070 US Route 1, York ME 03909, (207) 363-9363, est. 2002.

## Evidence on Hand

- Bullshirt facts: founded 2002, screen printing and embroidery, in-house samples, hours M-F 9-5 (Bullshirt engagement status file).
- Bullshirt-wide turnaround and pickup claims used in the Bayside test build: ships in two weeks or less, free in-store pickup. Treat as Bullshirt's standing promise, confirm per store.
- No testimonials, order counts, or school-specific photos exist. Any shown are synthetic and labeled.

## Product Principles

1. The action is the product: shop entry is visible in the first viewport, in working form.
2. York's identity leads; Bullshirt is the credited maker, not the brand.
3. Everything that cannot be ported into DecoNetwork's three theming surfaces is out of scope for the design.
4. Claims stay true: local, hand-printed, pickup. Nothing invented about volume, awards, or reviews.
